# Voice stage

- Default natural Persian female voice: `fa-IR-DilaraNeural` through `edge-tts` when online.
- Offline fallback: Persian Piper voice.
- Voice personality presets are available in Settings: playful/anime, caring, cool/serious, energetic, natural, plus custom rate/pitch/volume.
- Typed and reminder responses use the same speech path and avatar mouth pulse events.
- Japanese interjections remain Japanese in displayed text and are converted to Persian phonetic pronunciation for the Persian TTS path.
- Wellbeing reminders are non-blocking: 45 minutes active use -> break suggestion; 15 minutes idle -> occasional companion nudge.
